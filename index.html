About Me

I am Kaiwen Shi, a Ph.D. student at the University of Notre Dame. My research explores how language model agents can reason with structured knowledge, collaborate effectively, and express uncertainty more faithfully.

Previously, I studied Computer Science with a minor in Economics at Yuanpei College, Peking University.

Education

  • University of Notre Dame — Ph.D. student.
  • Peking University, Yuanpei College — Undergraduate degree in Computer Science, with a minor in Economics.

Research interests

  • Multi-agent collaboration: task-aware routing and adaptive coordination for question answering.
  • Uncertainty and alignment: uncertainty quantification, confidence calibration, and policy optimization.
  • Knowledge-grounded reasoning: knowledge graphs, retrieval, and multi-hop reasoning.
  • Trustworthy AI: citation verification, robust agent evaluation, and health and social-service applications.
