Language · Reasoning · Collaboration

Building more reliable
language model agents.

I am Kaiwen Shi, a Ph.D. student at the University of Notre Dame. My research explores how language model agents can reason with structured knowledge, collaborate effectively, and express uncertainty more faithfully.

Previously, I studied Computer Science with a minor in Economics at Yuanpei College, Peking University.

2026 research highlights

First-author papers at EACL and EMNLP, and co-first-author papers at ACL and ICML. Co-first authorship denotes equal contribution.

Research interests

Multi-agent collaboration

Task-aware routing, agent coordination, and adaptive collaboration for question answering.

Uncertainty & alignment

Uncertainty quantification, confidence calibration, and uncertainty-aware policy optimization.

Knowledge-grounded reasoning

Knowledge graphs and graph neural networks for retrieval and multi-hop reasoning.

Trustworthy AI

Citation verification, robust agent evaluation, and applications in health and social services.
